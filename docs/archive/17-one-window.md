# To do 17: One window at a time (small): "A second window hands its link or download to the first and closes"

- **Problem:** two downloads sent while the app is closed, faster than a new window starts listening on the pipe (about a second or two: two Downloads in the Chrome popup on two tabs, or a double-click on the button), open two windows. They download side by side, and the one that saves last writes history.json without the other's new rows. Also, a window opened while one is open already (from the Start menu or the terminal, no link) doesn't listen, and doesn't take over when the first one closes. Not seen by the user; found by the reviews of To do 10 step B and To do 16 step A (docs/research/browser-button.md, both "Review of step ..." points).
- **Goal:** there is only ever one window. Example: with the app closed, Download in the popup on two YouTube tabs one right after the other: one window opens, minimized, with both rows, the second waiting its turn.
- **Read first:** handoff.py `listen()` and `Listening.__init__` (the `PermissionError` when another window listens, now folded into `None` with every other `OSError`); app.py `App.__init__` (the end, where it listens), `start()`, `main()`; host.py `answer()`.
- **Approach (suggested; the build session checks it):** before a window builds its widgets, it tries to listen. When another window listens already (`PermissionError`), it hands its link, or its link and choices, to that one with `handoff.send()` and quits without showing anything. Whichever window makes the pipe first wins, so a race between two starting windows ends with one too. `listen()` has to tell `PermissionError` apart from a pipe that can't be made (a window opens then, as now). The tests and the GUI test pass their own pipe or none, so they don't change.
- **Ask the user:** (a) a window opened with no link while one is open: bring the open one to the front and close (a message with no link, e.g. `{"show": true}`), or open a second window as now? Suggested: bring the open one to the front. (b) If handing over fails at that moment (the first window is closing), open a window of its own? Suggested: yes, as `main()` does now.
- **Test:** Claude: app tests that a second window started on a busy pipe hands over its link and its download and opens nothing, and that a pipe that can't be made still opens a window; the existing handoff tests. User: with the app closed, Download in the popup on two tabs quickly: one window with both rows; start the app from the terminal while it's open: the open window comes to the front (if (a) is yes).
