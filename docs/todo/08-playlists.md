# To do 8: Playlists and whole channels (medium): "Playlists and whole channels"

- **Goal:** a playlist or channel link downloads all its videos. Today it takes only the first and the window says so. Example: a short YouTube playlist (`youtube.com/playlist?list=...`).
- **Read first:** `options()` (`noplaylist`, `playlist_items`) and `download()` in downloader.py; `PLAYLIST_NOTE` and `done_message()` in app.py; docs/decisions.md To do 3 ("a playlist or channel link downloads one video"); docs/research/yt-dlp-library.md "A page with two videos is a playlist".
- **Approach:** no `playlist_items` limit for playlist links; the progress line says "Video 3 of 12"; one failed video doesn't stop the rest, and the end message counts what was saved, skipped and failed.
- **Cancel** (decided in To do 13): stops the rest and keeps the videos already finished. The clean-up deletes new files named after the video in progress (`remove_unfinished()` with the hooks' `prefixes`), so `prefixes` must be emptied when a video finishes (a post hook), or Cancel would delete the finished ones too.

**Answers (Claude's suggestions, accepted before the to-do was moved):**
1. A subfolder for each playlist: `Downloads\Videos\<playlist name>\`, the name cut to 80 bytes (`%(playlist_title|.).80B/` in front of the file name). A single video still goes straight into Downloads\Videos. Downloading the same playlist again fetches only its new videos.
2. Above 50 videos, a Windows Yes/No box: "“Name - Videos” has 1,234 videos. Download all of them?" No downloads nothing. It comes only after yt-dlp has listed the whole playlist (for a big channel, an estimated tens of seconds; not measured).
3. `watch?v=...&list=...` gets just that video, as now (`noplaylist: True` stays).
4. A channel's home link gets yt-dlp's default: one playlist per tab (Videos, Shorts, Live), each in its own folder. For videos only, paste the Videos tab link (`youtube.com/@name/videos`). Rejected: rewriting channel links to `/videos` (YouTube-only code in the app).
5. Progress: "Video 3 of 12: Downloading video: 45% of 23 MB"; the bar fills once per video; between videos "Video 4 of 12: looking it up...".
6. End message: "Done: 10 of 12 videos saved, 1 was already there, 1 failed (in Downloads\Videos\My playlist). Why the first failed: <plain message>." Normal color; red only when nothing could be saved ("None of the 12 videos could be downloaded. Why the first failed: ..."). Other failures' reasons go to the terminal only.
7. Play plays the playlist's first video; Show in folder shows it selected in the playlist's folder.
8. Cancel during a playlist: "Cancelled. Kept the 3 videos already finished (in Downloads\Videos\My playlist)."; Play and Show in folder then work on those. After a cancelled single video, as now.
9. Subtitles: each video gets its own `.en.srt`; the end message adds "No English subtitles for 4 of the videos." when some have none.
